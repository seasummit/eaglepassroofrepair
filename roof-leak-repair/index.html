Roof leak repair in Eagle Pass

A small leak rarely stays small. Water that gets past the roof covering soaks the decking, the insulation and then the ceiling drywall, and the stain you see inside is usually some distance from where the water actually gets in.

Common causes of leaks on Eagle Pass homes

  • Shingles cracked, curled or blown off by strong wind
  • Worn flashing around chimneys, vents, skylights and wall joints
  • Dried-out or cracked rubber boots around plumbing vents
  • Ponding water on flat or low-slope roofs
  • Loose screws and worn washers on metal roofs
  • Hail impacts that break or bruise shingles

What to do while you wait

  • Put a bucket under the drip and move furniture and electronics away.
  • If the ceiling is bulging with water, keep people out of the room underneath.
  • Take photos of the ceiling and any damage you can see from the ground. Do not climb onto a wet roof.
  • Write down when the leak started and what the weather was.
