Metal roof repair in Eagle Pass

Metal roofs are common in Eagle Pass and the rest of Maverick County because they handle heat and sun well. They still need attention: the fasteners, seals and trim usually fail long before the metal panels do.

Common metal roof problems

  • Screws backing out or rubber washers cracking in the heat, letting water in at each hole
  • Leaks at the ridge cap, valleys or where the roof meets a wall
  • Rust at cut edges, scratches or where different metals touch
  • Panels loosened or bent by high wind
  • Hail dents (often cosmetic, sometimes not)

Repair or replace?

Many metal roof leaks are fixed by replacing fasteners and resealing seams and trim, which costs far less than new panels. A roofer can tell you which applies after looking at it.
